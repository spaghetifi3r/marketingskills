## Why This Analogy Works

Every parent understands brushing teeth. They don't debate it, avoid it, or feel weird about it — it's just part of raising a healthy child. The goal of this content arc is to move spinal hygiene into that same mental category.

The analogy works on three levels:

1. **Prevention over reaction** — you don't brush teeth because they already hurt; you brush to prevent decay. Same with adjustments.
2. **Routine, not treatment** — brushing is a habit, not a medical event. This reframes chiropractic from "scary" to "normal."
3. **Parents feel responsible, not judged** — framing it as hygiene means a parent who brings their child in is being proactive, not reactive. That's a much easier sell than "your child might be injured."

> *"The moment you stop brushing your teeth, cavities build up. If you don't do the spinal hygiene, subluxations build up. It all makes sense."*
> — Dr. Danny Park

## Brand Voice Reminders for This Arc

- **Warm, not clinical.** This audience is parents, not medical professionals. Use plain language.
- **Proactive, not alarming.** The goal is to empower, not scare. Frame everything as "you can prevent this" not "your child is damaged."
- **Dr. Park is the guide, not the hero.** The parent is the hero — they're protecting their child. Dr. Park is the expert who helps them do that.
- **Always specific over vague.** "49 lbs of pressure at a 45-degree head tilt" beats "a lot of pressure."
