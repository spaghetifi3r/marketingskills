### How to Use These Outlines

Each outline follows the same 6-step structure:
1. **Strong Hook** — stops the scroll in the first 2 seconds
2. **Introduction** — who you are and why you're credible
3. **Restate the Title** — tell them exactly what this video covers
4. **Why Are You Talking About This?** — why it matters to them right now
5. **The Content** — the actual information (this is the bulk of the video)
6. **Conclusion + CTA** — wrap up and tell them what to do next

Dr. Park: read the talking points, not the exact words. These are prompts, not scripts. Speak naturally.
