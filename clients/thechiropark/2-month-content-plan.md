# The Chiro Park — 2-Month Instagram Content Plan
### 1 Hour Per Week · Batch Film Day · 5 Content Types

**Prepared for:** Dr. Danny Park & The Chiro Park Team
**Period:** 8 Weeks
**Platform:** Instagram (primary) — all content repurposes to Facebook + YouTube Shorts
**Recording:** 1 hour per week, filmed in one session

---

## The System at a Glance

**5 content types, 5 posts per week, 1 filming day.**

| Type | Format | Length | Purpose |
|------|--------|--------|---------|
| Tip of the Day | Reel | 15–30 sec | Daily value, habit-building |
| Educational | Reel | 60–90 sec | Authority + trust |
| Transformational | Reel or Carousel | 45–75 sec | Emotional proof |
| BTS | Reel | 20–40 sec | Authenticity + culture |
| Funny / Meme | Reel | 15–30 sec | Reach + shareability |

**Posting schedule:**
- Monday — Educational
- Tuesday — Tip of the Day
- Wednesday — BTS
- Thursday — Transformational
- Friday — Funny / Meme

---

## The 1-Hour Filming Run Sheet

Use this every single week. Same order, every time. Do not deviate — the routine is what makes it fast.

```
00:00 – 00:05   Setup & warm-up
                Camera on, mic clipped, Dr. Park reviews the week's topic card
                Staff sets up any props needed (phone, chair, pillow, etc.)

00:05 – 00:15   Tip of the Day (10 min)
                Film 2 quick tips back to back — 1–2 takes each
                Keep it punchy: one idea, 20 seconds, done

00:15 – 00:30   Educational Reel (15 min)
                One topic, 60–90 seconds
                Allow 2–3 takes — use the best one
                Dr. Park speaks direct to camera, no script — just talking points

00:30 – 00:40   Transformational Content (10 min)
                Option A: Dr. Park tells a patient story (no patient needed)
                Option B: Patient on camera with consent (pre-arranged)
                One take, natural — don't over-rehearse this one

00:40 – 00:50   BTS (10 min)
                Staff member films Dr. Park doing something real:
                adjusting, reviewing X-rays, talking to a patient, setting up
                No script — just capture 3–4 candid moments

00:50 – 01:00   Funny / Meme (10 min)
                Pick the trending audio in advance (before filming day)
                Film the concept in 2–3 takes — keep it light
                This is the fastest one — one joke, nail it, move on
```

**Who does what on filming day:**
- Dr. Park: on camera, talking points only (no memorizing scripts)
- Staff member 1: holds the phone / camera
- Staff member 2: watches the run sheet, keeps time, prompts next segment
- Anyone else: available for BTS shots or as a co-star in meme videos

## Pre-Filming Checklist (Do This 24 Hours Before)

- [ ] Print the week's topic card — one per filming day
- [ ] Pick the trending audio for the meme video (check Instagram Reels trending sounds)
- [ ] Confirm any patients needed for transformational content (get consent form signed)
- [ ] Charge camera/phone, confirm mic is working
- [ ] Clear the filming space — clean background, good lighting
- [ ] Staff assigned: camera holder, timekeeper, topic prompter

## Post-Filming Checklist (Do This Within 48 Hours)

- [ ] Edit all 5 clips (captions on every video — 85% watched on mute)
- [ ] Write captions using the bank above — customize with week's specifics
- [ ] Schedule in Buffer or Later per the Mon–Fri posting calendar
- [ ] Save raw footage to a shared folder — never delete originals

---

## Content Bank: Build Ahead

After Week 4, you will start having more content than you need for that week. This is intentional.

- **Bank evergreen clips** (tip of the day, educational) — they never expire
- **Use banked content** on weeks when filming is disrupted (holidays, staff illness, busy periods)
- **Goal by Week 8**: have 2 weeks of content banked at all times
